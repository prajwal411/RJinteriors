# Visual and mobile: rjinteriors.in

Score: 85/100

Status: completed in the main session. The visual check was interrupted by a usage limit after it captured the screenshots. The screenshots are in ../screenshots/ (desktop and mobile, fold and full page, for home, products, projects, contact and Courtyard by Marriott).

## What works
- Mobile home, above the fold (home-mobile-fold.png): you see the eyebrow "GRC and FRP manufacturer, Bangalore", the H1, the lead paragraph naming the products, and a large "Request a quote" button. The value proposition is clear in 3 seconds.
- The text is legible on the dark gradient hero, and tap targets (the Menu button, the CTAs) are large.
- Mobile products page, above the fold (products-mobile-fold.png): it shows the H1, an intro, and jump links to GRC products, FRP products and Compare the materials.

## Findings
- **Medium: no phone number or WhatsApp above the fold on mobile.** Indian B2B buyers often call rather than fill in a form. Add a tap-to-call or WhatsApp button to the mobile header (src/partials/header.html).
- **Medium: /products/ and the home hero show no real product photos.** They have drawings only, and the 61 real project photos live only under /projects/. Add one or two project photos to each product section (see also sxo.md).
- **Low: the "Scroll to walk through the factory" hero takes up the screen below the CTAs.** The "Skip the walk" link helps. Check that the first real content isn't more than one long scroll away on small phones.
