Our brands

RJIC is the manufacturing company behind five brands. Three focus on a material and two on a region, and all five are supplied from our facility in Bangalore.

Where everything is made

Products for all five brands are manufactured at the same facility in Bangalore, Karnataka, and supplied from there to project sites.

Whichever brand you found us through, you can contact RJIC directly about any GRC or FRP product.

Material brands

GRC Bangalore

Architectural GRC and GFRC products and solutions: jalis and screens, domes and minarets, columns, cornices, facade panels and custom pieces.

FRP Bangalore
