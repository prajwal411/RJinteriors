Industries we serve

Our products go into homes, offices, hotels, schools, places of worship and industrial sites. Here is where GRC and FRP are typically used in each.

Residential

Independent houses, villas and apartment buildings.

GRC gives a house a distinctive elevation without the weight of solid concrete or stone, and the same mould can repeat a detail across every floor.

Typical products

  • Jali screens
  • Columns and capitals
  • Balustrades
  • Cornices and window surrounds
  • FRP brackets and cornices
  • FRP planters

Commercial

Offices, retail, showrooms and mixed-use buildings.

On commercial buildings the facade does a lot of work: it shades the glazing, screens services and gives the building an identity.

Typical products

  • Facade panels and cladding
  • Fins for sun shading
  • Feature screens
  • FRP facade elements

Hospitality

Hotels, resorts, restaurants and event venues.

Hospitality projects call for ornament and landscape pieces as much as facades: an entrance that sets the tone, a courtyard screen, planters along a drive.

Typical products

  • Domes
  • Arches
  • Decorative columns
  • Courtyard screens
  • FRP planters
  • Sculptures

Institutional

Schools, colleges, hospitals and public buildings.

Institutional buildings need exteriors that last and are practical to look after, along with utilities such as water storage.

Typical products

  • Facade screens
  • Cladding
  • Columns
  • Cornices
  • FRP storage tanks
  • FRP gutters

Religious

Temples, mosques, churches and community halls.

Places of worship often have the most specific requirements of all: domes, minarets and ornament made to a particular tradition, scale and design.

Typical products

  • Domes and minarets
  • Arches
  • Jalis
  • Columns and capitals
  • Cornices
  • Custom ornament

Industrial

Factories, utilities and industrial sites.

In industrial settings, FRP is used mainly for its resistance to corrosion and water.

Typical products

  • FRP storage tanks
  • MS FRP tanks
  • FRP gutters
  • FRP cladding
