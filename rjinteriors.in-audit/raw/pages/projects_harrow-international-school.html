About the project

White balustrades, window surrounds and cornices set off the red-brick buildings of Harrow International School.

The balustrades follow the stepped garden terraces and the main stairs, and white columns and pediments mark the entrances.

What we made

  • Balustrades and railingsBalustrades along the terraces and stairs
  • Cornices and bracketsCornices and window surrounds on the brick facades
  • Columns and capitalsColumns and pediments at the entrances
