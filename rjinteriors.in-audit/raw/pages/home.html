Two materials, one factory

Both can take almost any shape. Where the piece goes decides which one suits it best, and we help you choose.

GRCGlass fibre reinforced concrete

Cement and sand reinforced with alkali-resistant glass fibre, cast in thin sections. It has the look of stone but weighs far less than solid concrete.

Made from

  • Cement
  • Sand
  • Water
  • Alkali-resistant glass fibre

Why choose it

  • Stone-like finish
  • Holds fine detail
  • Light for its size

GRC productsHow GRC is made

FRPFibre reinforced plastic

Resin reinforced with glass fibre, built up in a mould. It is light, water-tight and resists corrosion, so it suits tanks and planters as well as mouldings for high places.

Made from

  • Surface coat
  • Glass fibre mat
  • Resin

Why choose it

  • Light to lift and fix
  • Water-tight
  • Resists corrosion

Ordering, start to finish

Here is what happens after you get in touch.

  1. Share the design

    Send drawings, photos, sketches or a sample, with sizes, quantities and the site location.

  2. Agree the details

    We confirm material, dimensions, joints, fixings and finish with you or your architect, then quote.

  3. Develop the mould

    A mould is made for your design, so every piece cast from it matches.

  4. Make and finish

    Pieces are made at our Bangalore facility, then finished and checked against the agreed details.

  5. Deliver and install

    We deliver to your site, and install the pieces on projects where installation is offered.
