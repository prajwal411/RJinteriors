GRC products

Glass fibre reinforced concrete (GRC, also called GFRC) is cement and sand reinforced with alkali-resistant glass fibre. There is no steel inside to rust, it can be cast in thin sections that hold fine detail, and it has a stone-like finish. RJ Interiors & Constructions (RJIC) makes every GRC product below to order at its factory in Jakkur, Bangalore, and delivers it to project sites.

GRC jalis and screens

Perforated screens in geometric, floral or custom patterns. A jali filters strong sun, gives privacy without closing off a wall, and lets air move through.

FRP products

Fibre reinforced plastic (FRP) is resin reinforced with glass fibre, built up in a mould. It is light, watertight and resistant to corrosion, which suits tanks and planters as well as architectural pieces that need to stay light. RJIC moulds every FRP product below to order at its factory in Jakkur, Bangalore.

GRC or FRP?

Many shapes can be made in either material. In short: GRC has a stone-like finish and suits facades, jalis, domes and columns. FRP is lighter, watertight and resistant to corrosion, and suits tanks, gutters, planters and light mouldings for interiors and upper floors.

Read the full GRC vs FRP comparison on gfrcinnovation.in, or tell us where the piece will go and we will recommend one.
