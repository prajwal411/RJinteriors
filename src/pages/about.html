About RJ Interiors & Constructions

RJIC manufactures custom architectural products in glass fibre reinforced concrete (GRC) and fibre reinforced plastic (FRP) at its facility in Bangalore, Karnataka. It is the parent company of GRC Bangalore, FRP Bangalore, GRC Chennai and GRC Kochi.

{{head-drawing}}

What we do

We make architectural products to order for residential, commercial, industrial, institutional and religious projects. The range covers decorative pieces such as jalis, domes, columns and cornices; building elements such as facade panels, cladding and fins; and practical products such as FRP storage tanks and gutters.

Alongside manufacturing, we offer custom product design, mould development, fabrication and supply, and on-site installation where it is offered.

Made to order

Architectural elements are rarely standard. A dome has to sit on its drum, a jali has to suit its opening and the light it lets in, and a run of cornice has to match the building it finishes.

That is why customised manufacturing is the centre of our business: each product is developed around a project's drawings, dimensions and finish.

Manufacturing in Bangalore

Our manufacturing facility is in Bangalore, Karnataka. Products for all four of our brands are made there and supplied to project sites.

When you contact RJIC, you are talking to the manufacturer, so questions about design, production and delivery can be answered directly.

How we manufacture

Our commitment to quality

In custom work, quality comes from agreeing the details before production and checking them afterwards.

Details agreed first
Dimensions, profiles, finishes and fixing details are confirmed with you before a mould is made.
Material matched to the job
We recommend GRC or FRP based on where the piece goes and what it has to do.
Checked before dispatch
Finished pieces are inspected for dimensions and finish before they leave our facility.
Coordinated with your site
We keep you informed from approval to delivery, and plan dispatch around your site schedule.

Our brands

GRC Bangalore and FRP Bangalore each focus on one material. GRC Chennai and GRC Kochi serve customers in Tamil Nadu and Kerala. All four are supplied from our Bangalore facility.
