Our brands

RJIC is the manufacturing company behind four brands. Two focus on a material and two on a region, and all four are supplied from our facility in Bangalore.

{{head-drawing}}

Where everything is made

Products for all four brands are manufactured at the same facility in Bangalore, Karnataka, and supplied from there to project sites.

Whichever brand you found us through, you can contact RJIC directly about any GRC or FRP product.
