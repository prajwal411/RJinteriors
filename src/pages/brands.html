Our brands

RJ Interiors & Constructions (RJIC) is the manufacturing company behind five brands. Two focus on a material and three on a region, and every product all five offer is made at our factory in Jakkur, Bangalore.

{{head-drawing}}

Where everything is made

Products for all five brands are manufactured at the same factory in Jakkur, Bangalore, and delivered from there to project sites.

Whichever brand you found us through, you can contact RJIC directly about any GRC or FRP product.

Material brands

GRC Bangalore

GRC (also called GFRC) products for customers in Bangalore and across Karnataka: jalis and screens, domes and minarets, columns, cornices, facade panels and custom pieces.

Visit grcbangalore.com

FRP Bangalore

FRP products and custom applications: storage tanks and MS FRP tanks, planters, moulded columns, brackets, corbels and cornices, facade elements, gutters and decorative pots.

Visit frpbangalore.com

Regional brands

GRC Chennai

Our regional GRC brand for customers in Chennai and across Tamil Nadu. Products are made at our Jakkur factory and delivered to project sites in the region.

Visit grcchennai.co.in

GRC Kochi

Our regional GRC brand for customers in Kochi and across Kerala. Products are made at our Jakkur factory and delivered to project sites in the region.

Visit grckochi.in

GFRC Innovation

Our brand for GRC and FRP customers in the rest of India, outside Karnataka, Tamil Nadu and Kerala. Its website also has a full comparison of GRC and FRP.
