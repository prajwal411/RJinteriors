Our brands

RJ Interiors & Constructions (RJIC) is the manufacturing company behind five brands. Two focus on a material and three on a region, and their products are made in Bangalore.

{{head-drawing}}

Where everything is made

Products for all five brands are made in Bangalore. GRC is made at our unit in Jakkur, and FRP at FRP Bangalore's own factory. Both are delivered from Bangalore to project sites.

Whichever brand you found us through, you can contact RJIC directly about any GRC or FRP product.

Material brands

GRC Bangalore

GRC (also called GFRC) products for customers across Karnataka: jalis and screens, domes and minarets, columns, cornices, facade panels and custom pieces.

Visit grcbangalore.com

FRP Bangalore

FRP products for customers across South India: storage tanks and MS FRP tanks, planters, moulded columns, brackets, corbels and cornices, facade elements, gutters and decorative pots.

Visit frpbangalore.com

Regional brands

GRC Chennai

Our regional GRC brand for customers in Tamil Nadu and Puducherry. Products are made in Bangalore and delivered to project sites in the region.

Visit grcchennai.co.in

GRC Kochi

Our regional GRC brand for customers across Kerala. Products are made in Bangalore and delivered to project sites in the region.

Visit grckochi.in

GFRC Innovation

Our brand for GRC and FRP customers in the rest of India, outside the regions the other four brands cover.

Visit gfrcinnovation.in
